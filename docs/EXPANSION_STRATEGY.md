# All Over Nepal — expansion strategy

**Owner-directed 2026-10-04:** "go deep, make us all over Nepal or what's
best... coding is 0 investment here rn... we must grow and profit insanely."

## The advantage

Code costs ~$0 right now. A normal startup burns tens of thousands
learning what we learn free. So we build aggressively — but **only
where the research points**. Free code doesn't excuse random code.

## The map (from 12 rounds of research)

- **Taken:** ledgers (Karobar — 500k+ installs, profitable, 35 people),
  payments (Fonepay / eSewa / Khalti). Stay out.
- **Open:** response capacity, payment verification. Nobody there.
- **Price:** outcome-based, or ~Rs 2,000/yr (Karobar proved it). Thin
  wallets can't do per-seat.
- **Distribution:** trust networks + creators. Not app stores, not ads —
  owners distrust vendors and learn from peers.
- **Moat:** Nepali localization (Bikram Sambat calendar), the needs
  data asset, the trust graph itself.

"Less competition" is true in the open corners, false in the taken ones.
We expand only through open corners.

## Phases

1. **First rupee (now):** the sprint — 1 merchant, 1 week,
   human-powered. The inbox prototype (built this cycle) is the
   delegate's demo and week tool.
2. **Playbook (weeks):** 10 merchants via the trust network — every
   served merchant introduces peers. Prototype becomes tested workflow.
   Target: ₨10,000.
3. **Product (months):** the inbox becomes merchant self-serve software
   at Rs 2,000/yr. Target: ₨100,000.
4. **Everywhere (year+):** Kathmandu → valley → Terai → all Nepal. Then
   the Karobar playbook: the model travels (Bangladesh proved it).
   Nepali-originated, world-facing — no geographic priority, ever.

## The math of "insanely"

- 5,000 merchants × Rs 2,000/yr = **Rs 10M/yr** (Karobar's proven number,
  not a fantasy).
- CAC ≈ 0 (trust networks, not ads) · churn ≈ 0 (outcome-based pricing —
  they pay from value created) · margins ≈ 100% (zero-cost code).
- 50,000 merchants = Rs 100M/yr. That's the insane. It starts with one
  merchant and one recovered sale.

## Guardrails (from our own postmortems)

- **Tootle:** never pioneer a category that needs regulatory education.
  Our wedge (reply-handling, verification aids) needs no regulator. If
  we ever touch lending or payments rails, we partner — we don't
  educate.
- **Never fake:** the prototype is labeled prototype; empty states stay
  empty until a real week fills them. No demo data, no phantom metrics.
- **Build follows validation:** inbox v1 after the sprint proves it;
  verification aid after merchants confirm D19 in their own words. The
  engine's gate applies to the roadmap too.

## Build order (code is free — in this order)

2. Merchant inbox v1 — after the first rupee lands.
3. Payment-verification aid — after D19 is confirmed by real voices.
4. Everything else ordered by verified needs, never by excitement.
