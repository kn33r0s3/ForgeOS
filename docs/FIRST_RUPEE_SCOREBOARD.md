## Evidence rules

A send is not a conversation; a conversation is not a sale; a seller assertion is not payment evidence. A recovered sale may be marked VERIFIED only when the record establishes a traceable previously missed inquiry, seller confirmation that the sale otherwise would likely have been lost, a completed order, seller-consented evidence of completion/payment, and an owner-reviewed evidence reference. Otherwise classify it as REPORTED or UNKNOWN.

Record any future allowed event through the existing Hami evidence/commitment path, minimizing data and retaining no customer names, numbers, messages, screenshots or account credentials in the public repository.
