# First Rupee Sprint

**Aim (owner-set 2026-10-04):** find real value ASAP. Nepal is small,
developing, and early — the competition gap is real but *specific*.
This doc names the specific gap and the fastest test.

## Where the gap actually is

From 12 rounds of research, the merchant-software map of Nepal:

- **Taken:** ledgers (Karobar — 500k+ installs, profitable, 35 people),
  payments (Fonepay/eSewa/Khalti).
- **Open:** response capacity (nobody sells it), adversarial payment
  verification (nobody solves it).

"Less competition" is true in the open corners and false in the taken
ones. We do not compete where a profitable Nepali company already won.

## The target: missed inquiries (need #1, confidence: high)

Social sellers lose sales to slow replies — phone is identity, a missed
message is a missed sale, and owners themselves say they "can't keep up."
This is the fastest path to the first rupee because:

1. **Measurable in days** — count missed inquiries for one week.
2. **No build** — a human handles replies; $0 tech.
3. **No bespoke product build for discovery** — a human can study the reply-capacity problem before software is built. This is not a claim of regulatory exemption. The Department lists the Electronic Business (E-Commerce) Act, 2081 and an official “Urgent Notice Regarding Mandatory Listing” dated 2 Bhadra, 2083. Hami's legal position is unresolved; see D72 in docs/UNKNOWN_MAP.md.
4. **Outcome-based pricing is a hypothesis, not an approved term.** Price/revenue share is owner-only. Do not propose a cut, fixed fee, “free test,” paid service, or fee collection until qualified Nepali legal/accounting review covers seller eligibility, Hami's role, contracts, privacy/data handling, tax and payment rails, and the owner authorizes exact commercial terms. A discovery interview may proceed only under existing contact/consent rules.

## Discovery before any operational sprint

1. Conduct a consented, owner-authorized discovery conversation first. Ask what happens to inquiries and neutrally ask what registration/listing steps they have completed. Do not pitch a service or infer legal compliance from a verbal answer.
2. Count baseline inquiries and replies only if the seller voluntarily supplies the information and approves its use. Do not copy customer messages, names, phone numbers, order contents or credentials into Hami.
3. A paid/operational week is **not authorized by this document**. It requires evidence that the seller meets eligibility rules confirmed by counsel; a recorded legal/accounting answer on Hami's intermediary/platform role, contracts, privacy/data processing, tax and payment collection; explicit seller permission for channel/account access and response behavior; and a separate owner ASK approving exact price, terms, spend and customer-facing actions.
4. Only after all gates are recorded can an owner-authorized sprint measure reply time, recovered sales and payment evidence. A match, interview, estimate or reported sale is not a REAL sale/payment.
5. If a gate cannot be cleared, continue non-commercial discovery or test a different lawful value path. Do not treat software, draft offers or seller interest as legal clearance.

## What moves the number

- 1 recovered sale at any price → thesis alive, first rupee possible.
- 0 recoverable inquiries in a week → the need is weaker than observed;
  kill or re-target (need #3, paid-proof, is next).
- Merchant won't pay for recovered sales → pricing/positioning problem,
  not a need problem; re-examine.

## What this is not

Not intake activation or permission to contact people or handle their messages. It is a proposed wedge hypothesis with a one-week measure, still subject to owner-only pricing, legal and customer-action gates. Candidate → verified → served → **paid** only counts when each stage actually happens and is evidenced.
