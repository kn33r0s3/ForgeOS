# Forge Bot owner-run discovery and baseline

Status: **HYPOTHESIS / TEST TEMPLATE.** The Nepal education-abroad
consultancy segment is unvalidated. This sheet has no prospect/customer
evidence. The owner conducts about five real conversations with licensed
agencies; ForgeOS must not contact them or simulate conversations.

## Conversation guide

Ask permission to take minimized notes. Do not collect student names, phone
numbers, passport numbers, identity documents, or private case histories.
Record only an opaque prospect reference and the business-level facts the
owner actually reports.

1. How many new inquiries do you receive in a typical week? Which channels
   generated them? How do you know?
2. What is the current time to first response by channel? What happens after
   hours or on busy days?
3. What steps and facts do you use to decide whether an inquiry is a fit?
   Which facts are required, and which are sensitive or inappropriate to ask?
4. How many follow-ups are normally sent, on what schedule, and when do you
   stop?
5. How does a qualified person book a consultation today? What proves that a
   booking actually happened?
6. Which tools are already used for inbox, messaging, scheduling, and status
   tracking? What integrations are permitted by their terms?
7. Where does the owner/team lose the most time? What errors or missed leads
   occur, and how often?
8. What is a converted lead worth to the agency, and what records support that
   estimate? Do not treat a stated value as revenue evidence.
9. Which approved factual answers can automation use? What topics must always
   go to a person?
10. What channel would they authorize first, under what opt-in, quiet-hour,
    STOP, retention, and escalation rules?

Do not pitch, quote a price, publish an offer, or promise results in these
discovery conversations unless the owner explicitly authorizes that action
beforehand. Do not promise admission, visa, employment, or conversion outcomes.

## Adaptation gate

Do not implement universal qualification fields or channel assumptions from
this hypothesis. After five conversations, summarize what was actually
reported in `docs/REVENUE_LOG.md`; adjust the Forge Bot spec only to recurring,
owner-confirmed needs. If no repeated pain is evidenced, stop or change the
segment/offer rather than adding technology. Customer, payment, and delivery
claims require their own evidence in `docs/REVENUE_LOG.md`.

## Pilot terms checklist — do not activate without owner approval

Before a pilot, record the actual agreed price, term, success metric, data
ownership, automated-assistant disclosure, opt-out handling, retention/deletion
path, channel permission, escalation boundary, and payment evidence method.
Payment evidence is an owner-recorded bank or wallet reference. Keep eSewa and
Khalti adapters locked until a real payment occurs. Do not add a billing system.
