# Forge — end-state blueprint

This is the ceiling. It is the system Forge is being built toward, written the way an engineering team writes the final map before the work, including parts that are not possible yet. Nothing in this file is a claim that it already exists. Nothing in this file is permission to invent records, ratings, prices, payments, or emergencies.

Today’s running system is the seed of this one system. The blueprint does not replace it with a second product.

---

ForgeOS is not merely a registry of what exists. It continuously searches for economically and socially useful connections that could exist, checks them against evidence and constraints, and helps authorized parties make them real.

The public service path is the first usable wedge. It is not the identity of Forge. Do not rename the system after that wedge, and do not replace it with another single purpose such as a job board or a trade board. A job is work a person or organization can perform or needs performed. A service is one form of that work. Nepal is the first geography. The network — people, organizations, capabilities, needs, work, offers, trades, evidence, trust, and outcomes — is the asset underneath.

Nobody does meaningful work for Forge until the post states the economic contract: what is requested, who may submit, what counts as success, what evidence is required, who owns the result, when payment is due, how much, what happens on partial completion, what happens on rejection, whether the result may be resold, and who bears the costs. Rejection pays nothing unless those terms say otherwise. A missing contract blocks acceptance. It does not block stating the need.

## 1. The finished thing

Forge is our own world of record for real life and real value, starting in Nepal and able to see the rest of the world’s asset and opportunity surface.

People come because the useful things are here and stay current:

- find work and offer work
- find a service and offer a service
- post an offer, answer an offer, trade
- see what changed nearby
- see a real opportunity the machine found, with the evidence under it
- get paid, and have that payment be a record, not a guess

A person does not have to live inside the site. Forge keeps operating. When something relevant actually changes, the person hears about that change and responds. The site is the place they enter the world. The machine is what makes the world worth entering. On the work page that path is one surface: describe a need, see the recorded matches, open trust for a verified provider, record a request, and follow its status. Alerts and the event timeline on that page show only changes already stored.

The public face stays plain. Search, verified providers, listings, jobs, offers, trades, booking, status, and later payment and dispute. People do not see experiments, internal scores, operator notes, private evidence, or another person’s private details.

Under that face, one engine continuously does this:

```text
OBSERVE
  → EVIDENCE
  → RESEARCH
  → UNDERSTAND
  → MATCH / DECIDE
  → AUTHORIZED ACTION
  → EXECUTE
  → REAL RESPONSE
  → TRANSACTION / EVENT
  → OUTCOME
  → LEARNING
  → UPDATED WORLD
  → again
```

That loop is the brain. It is not a chatbot placed on top of a database. It is shared state, evidence, relationships, policy, action, outcome, and learning. A feature is finished only when it consumes something the world already knows, produces new evidence the rest of the world can use, or connects two sides that could not find each other.

---

The network is worldwide: work, resources, supply, demand, and transfers. Nepalese people should be able to earn from it and help Nepal develop. The candidate space is already global in scope. Forge does not pre-load that space with listings. A possible connection stays internal and huge. A proposal is smaller, because it requires a cited evidence reference. Agreement, fulfillment, and payment are smaller still. Billions of possibilities are not billions of fake listings.

## 2. The world Forge owns

Forge’s domain is the set of records that exist because something happened here, or because Forge gathered evidence and kept the provenance.

The world eventually holds:

And the larger value surface the machine researches, without pretending a headline number is a measurement:

```text
equities

venture capital
```

Knowledge is knowledge no matter who produced it, where it was published, or how it arrived. A paper, a forum, a price list, a person's post, and a record Forge already holds can all be used to research, match, and create. Origin does not decide whether something may be used. Origin decides the epistemic state and what Forge is allowed to claim about it.

Forge may use a source to build something new. Forge may not pretend that source is a verified fact, a verified provider, or a payment. A rounded global total copied from an article is still only that article's claim. If the source later disappears, the copy Forge stored, and the work Forge did with it, remain.

## 1.0 Speed

Forge should be fast at turning a real change into a correct, authorized outcome. Known facts plus valid authorization are the fast path. Unknown, stale, or high-impact situations are the careful path: gather evidence, then act. A fast wrong decision is worse than a slow correct one. The measure is the time from a stored connection to a recorded payment, not requests per second. That duration is reported only after a payment outcome exists.

## 1.1 Expansion law

ForgeOS only gets stronger. A working capability stays. New work connects more of the world to it, then adds what the network actually needs, then connects that back into the same evidence, action, outcome, and learning loop.

Learn from, reuse, interoperate with, and license what already exists. Do not steal, infringe, deceive, or take what was not authorized. Do not silently break a path that already works.

Utilized means a valuable capability has a real consumer: a public surface, the internal cockpit, or the cycle itself. It does not mean every endpoint gets its own page. Machine-only paths stay machine-only.

## 2.1 Mediator

Forge does not have to own, hold, or be the thing people want. It can stand between the sides. Someone is buying, someone else can supply, and the engine introduces them, keeps the evidence, and records what happened. Selling what people are buying does not mean Forge must possess that thing.

A match, a connection, or a completed trade is between the parties. Forge's part is the introduction, the record, and, when a real amount is stored, the note of that amount. Forge does not become the owner because it helped. It does not assume permission to use a skill, a vehicle, a stock of goods, or a workplace. Use is allowed only when the records say so.

This current machinery is a sliver of that. The ceiling is the engine doing this across work, services, trades, and the wider asset surface, again and again, from whatever knowledge exists.

## 3. Why a person returns

The aim is not a visit counter. The aim is that ordinary life keeps producing a reason to use Forge, and that Forge is already ready when the reason appears.

```text
morning        find work that matches a real skill and a real place
later          find a mechanic, a part, a supplier
later          post something for sale or trade
later          see a change in price, supply, or demand that matters to them
a business     find a worker, a customer, capacity, or a missing input
```

Many of those moments should not require the person to remember to open the site. The machine notices a relevant change and brings it. The person acts when it is worth acting.

“Must use” does not mean a lock-in trick. It means the coordination, the evidence, and the record of what happened live here, so doing it somewhere else throws away the benefit.

## 4. One machine

There is one Forge.

```text
public world          what people see and do
        │
        ▼

        │
        ├── records we own
```

Agents and outside models are workers. They may help build the machine or read a source. They do not own a second database, a second public site, a second worker, or a second definition of “verified”. When they are gone, the cycle, the records, and the learning remain.


New capability extends one of those, or adds a record inside this same world. It does not stand up a parallel product beside them.

## 5. Evidence law

Every important statement can answer:

```text
Where did this come from?
When was it observed?
What evidence supports it?
How fresh is it?
Has it been independently confirmed?
What action depended on it?
What outcome followed?
```

The world distinguishes:

```text
FACT              confirmed by a record or an independent check
OBSERVATION       something a collector or a person saw
EXTERNAL CLAIM    something a source said
INFERENCE         something Forge concluded
UNKNOWN           not known
STALE             known once, no longer fresh
```

Research produces questions, evidence, and hypotheses. It does not silently become a public fact. An AI judgment does not verify a business, a price, a payment, or an emergency. Verification is a stored record: evidence type, evidence reference, reviewer, time. Publication is a separate authorized step.

Money is the same law. Forge may say “this payment event was recorded, amount Y, tied to transaction Z.” Forge may not say “this probably generated a large profit.” A successful HTTP call, a sent email, or a posted offer is not revenue.

## 6. The discovery engine

This is the part that makes the public world worth opening.

Forge does not wait to be handed every lead. On each cycle it looks at its own world and at the sources it is allowed to read, and it asks what it does not yet know.

The curiosity scan already turns weak beliefs, contradictions, untested ideas, and ungrounded opportunities into research questions. It also opens a standing research agenda: a fixed list of evidence-seeking questions about Nepal (work, services, trade, housing, money, infrastructure) and the wider asset surface. The same question is never stored twice. The planner turns a question into tasks. Each daily cycle then collects a small bounded batch of those tasks. What comes back is an observation. It is not a public fact, not a price Forge invented, and not a verification.

The agenda is the research map, still inside that same scan, covering:

```text
Nepal, first
  work available and work needed
  services and parts
  local trade and supply gaps
  places where demand and supply do not meet

Then the asset surface
  each class in section 2
  as questions about current public evidence
  never as a copied global valuation treated as truth
```

A discovery is a linked object, not a paragraph:

```text
signal or source document
    → claim, with epistemic state
    → evidence
    → research question, if something is missing
    → opportunity, if the existing economic tests pass
    → authorized action, if a person or policy allows it
    → public post, only when publication rules pass
    → outcome, after reality answers
    → lesson
```

The machine also looks for missing links inside its own world:

```text
a provider with no nearby requests
a skill with no matching work
a job with no candidate
an offer with no counterparty
a resource in one place and demand in another
a price or supply change that touches an open need
```

Finding the gap is research. Filling it in public is an authorized action. The two are not the same step.

What people eventually see is the useful residue: a verified provider, an open job, a stated offer, a trade, a cited change, a match that names what is known and what is still unknown. They do not see the internal score that ranked it.

The first public slice of that residue is `/discoveries`. It lists a claim only when existing evidence ties that claim to an external signal with a source address. The epistemic state shown is the claim's own state (`observed`, `supported`, or `contested`). Internal scores and confidence stay off the response. A signal with no claim does not appear. An empty page means that chain has not been stored yet.

## 7. The network

```text
people
  ↕ skills, needs, location
jobs and offers and trades
  ↕ stated terms, city, price only if stated
providers and services
  ↕ verification, listing, availability
transactions
  ↕ booking, acceptance, fulfillment, payment, dispute
evidence
  ↕ source, time, freshness, confirmation
Forge
  ↕ matches, decisions, lessons
```

Each new real event makes the next match less blind. That is the network effect worth having. Vanity counts are not. The measures that matter are correctness, freshness, traceability, completed real outcomes, less friction, less uncertainty, and safety.

Trust is accumulated behavior, not a generated star rating:

```text
identity
  → verification evidence
  → service and job history
  → completion, cancellation, dispute, timeliness
  → payment events
  → updated trust
```

A provider or a counterparty becomes trustworthy because those events exist. Until they exist, the public page says they do not.

## 8. Action and money

Forge may discover, draft, and propose. External contact, verification, and publication stay behind the approval gate that already exists.

```text
candidate            recorded, not public
verification         evidence + reviewer, still not public
publication          separate approval
booking or response  a real request, not an acceptance
payment              a recorded amount, or it is not a payment
outcome              what happened, including failure
lesson               one event does not prove a market
```

The economic loop at the ceiling:

```text
need or discovered gap
  → match
  → authorized introduction or post
  → response
  → fulfillment
  → verified payment and settlement
  → reputation from that event
  → lesson
  → better next match
```

Jobs, services, offers, and trades all end in that same outcome record. A closed post without a stated amount is not revenue. A paid close stores the amount the participants recorded.

Later, settlement can sit in this loop. It is still a recorded transfer tied to a transaction. It is not a forecast.

## 9. Matching

A stored connection moves one state at a time: candidate, evidenced, viable, proposed, authorized, contacted, accepted, fulfilled, paid, completed. Evidenced requires a fresh stored source. Viable records only quotes that appear in that source, or leaves the figure unknown. None of those states is an agreement. A failed path stays failed and does not delete the need. Another candidate may be opened. The public list shows a connection only after it is authorized and explicitly published. There is no score. A proposal is refused unless its evidence reference is an external observation already stored, and that observation is still fresh. Older evidence stays visible and is labeled stale. It cannot promote a candidate. Authorization may store a landed cost, a margin, a buyer, or a route only when that phrase already appears in the stored evidence. A missing figure stays null. Forge does not calculate it. The internal Network page lists every connection, including the ones that stay off the public site. The internal Actions page lists execution actions and blocked actions, and sends approval to the existing backend endpoint. The page does not decide policy itself. The internal Research page lists stored questions and collection tasks. A question on that page is not a public fact. The internal Outcomes page lists stored outcomes and learning events. Writing a number there does not create goods, money, or wealth. The internal Runtime page lists stored record counts, the last cycle status, queued worker tasks, and cycle history. A count is not a claim that the world outside is healthy.

Forge can coordinate people, capabilities, assets, needs, and exchange. It can introduce sides that do not belong to Forge. It cannot turn a recorded number into real resources. A unit of account inside the network is not universally accepted money, and it is not the place to start.

The first match is a read across every kind of knowledge already stored. An open job, offer, or trade is compared with verified public providers, other open posts, and external claims that share words with the need. A provider match can be acted on as a counterparty. A knowledge match is someone else's statement, labeled with its epistemic state, and it is not a counterparty and not a completed trade. Stated price and availability are copied only when a record states them. Anything missing is named unknown. No match score is published.

Matching is not a second product. It is a question the same world can answer:

```text
Who can satisfy this need?
Where?
When?
On what stated terms?
What evidence supports the match?
What is still unknown?
```

Quality moves when outcomes come back. A model’s confidence is not a match score. A match with thin evidence is shown as thin.

## 10. Independence

At the ceiling, Forge’s own store holds:

```text
the world records
the evidence and its age
the decisions and who authorized them
the actions and their results
the payments and disputes
the lessons
```

Outside inputs that may exist, and may disappear:

```text
web, news, research papers, discussion sites
payment rails
identity checks
maps
language models used as workers
```

Losing an input degrades discovery. It does not delete the world. The cycle keeps running on what Forge already holds, marks stale claims as stale, and keeps serving the records people created here.

The operating shape:

```text
start Forge
  → API is up
  → the canonical cycle is up
  → research tasks run
  → public world and internal cockpit read the same records
```

One scheduler. One database. One public API for the world people touch. The internal cockpit is for operators. It is not a second network.

## 11. Safety, much later

The same evidence machine can coordinate confirmed high-stakes events: a verified incident, an area, people and supplies, capacity, transport, and a confirmed outcome.

That layer is not built by guessing. It requires:

```text
an explicit evidence threshold
uncertainty visible on the record
no invented emergency
no medical prediction from a model
no high-risk action without a matching authorization
an audit of who acted
a lesson when the action fails
```

Until that threshold exists, Forge does not speak as an emergency system.

## 12. Map from today to the ceiling

What is already real, and must not be thrown away:

```text
public search, verified provider, listing, booking, private status
booking and recorded offer-closure written into outcomes
job / offer / trade posts in our own domain records
action types that create a candidate, verify from cited evidence, and publish only after approval
the cycle scheduler around forge_loop
research questions, tasks, and collectors
claims with an epistemic state
outcomes and lessons that refuse estimated cash
```

What the ceiling still requires, in order, inside this same machine:

```text
1. Cycle stays the single continuous runner.
2. Standing research agenda is open. Each cycle collects a bounded
   batch. Evidence stays an observation until the publication rules pass.
3. Discoveries link signal → claim → question → opportunity
   without skipping the evidence rules.
4. The public world shows those discoveries that pass publication rules,
   and hides internal scores.
5. Matching uses real records and says what is unknown.
6. Payment and dispute are recorded events. A connection can enter
   `paid` only from `fulfilled`, and only with an amount that changed
   hands. That record stays reported until a separate confirmation
   marks it verified. A verified or reported payment can be disputed.
   The dispute names no winner. A settlement exists only when a new
   amount and a note are stated. Trust lists reported, verified,
   disputed, and settled payments separately. Each dispute and
   settlement writes a learning event. A dispute stores the note and names no winner.
   Reputation is `GET /public/trust/{kind}/{id}`: the recorded requests,
   reported payments, verified payments, and disputes. It is not a score.
7. A public alert exists only when an outcome was already recorded
   for a post, a booking, or a connection. A reply is one of those
   outcomes and does not count as acceptance. No change, no alert.
8. A later safety layer uses the same evidence law, or it does not ship.
```

Each step is done only when the chain can be shown on the real database: data, service, cycle, action, authorized effect, outcome, lesson.

## 13. What “finished” means

Forge is finished enough when a person in Nepal can find work, hire help, post an offer, trade, and get a true status, while the machine has already been reading the world and can show a sourced opportunity the person did not have to discover alone.

It is at the ceiling when that same machine also watches the wider asset surface, keeps every important claim traceable, learns from real completions and real payments, and still tells the truth when it does not know.

The impossible-looking part — one world that spans a neighborhood repair and a global commodity gap, and gets more useful every time either one actually happens — is the point of the map. It is reached by growing this Forge, not by starting another one.

## 14. The 200-day work order

`TWO_HUNDRED_DAY_COPILOT.txt` is the task queue for this map. It is not a second plan. One task is one session. The phases follow section 12:

1. The public API stays the existing FastAPI app, and a write survives a second request. On Vercel that requires `DATABASE_URL`. `/tmp/forge.db` is not the world of record.
2. The verified service path, bookings, and honest empty lists.
3. Needs, gaps, and lawful public sources. A headline is not an open job.
4. Canonical beliefs, then the cockpit that reads them.
5. The one scheduler and bounded collectors.
6. Trust, alerts, disputes, and payments as records.
7. One real pilot only after a person acts.
8. Stop. No new product after the last task.

Publicity stays shut until `docs/PUBLICITY_GATE.md` is checked from command output. A live health JSON is not a launch.
