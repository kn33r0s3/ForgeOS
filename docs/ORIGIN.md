# Origin positioning: Nepali-originated, world-facing

Standing product principle (owner-set, 2026-10-04): Hami must look and be
Nepali-originated — built in Kathmandu, representing Nepal worldwide.
Nepal-first is not a stepping stone; it is the identity. Global intent
follows from it, not instead of it.

## Why this is strategy, not decoration

Discovery rounds 1–2 found the opening directly: every tool sold to "small
businesses" was designed for someone in San Francisco. Nepali merchants
live in conversational commerce (call-first, phone-number-as-identity,
missed-call-is-missed-order), cash on delivery, Viber/WhatsApp-first
inquiries, and a paperwork wall with no informal digital tier. A product
shaped by that reality is structurally different from a translated
San Francisco tool — and that difference is the moat.

## Expressions (in order of importance)

1. **Behavior.** The product must work the way Nepali merchants work:
   conversational, phone-first, tolerant of informality, honest about
   cash. If it behaves like a San Francisco SaaS with a Nepali flag on
   top, it is costume — and merchants will smell it instantly.
2. **Name.** Hami (हामी — "we/us") already flies the flag. Keep it
   central; Devanagari belongs in the wordmark, not as decoration.
3. **Language.** Nepali-first where merchants read; English where the
   world reads. Never machine-translated marketing Nepali — a native
   speaker owns every public Nepali word.
4. **Story.** "Built in Kathmandu from real merchant reality, for the
   world's merchants." Open-source, built in public, documented honestly.
   The world-facing part is proof of quality, not dilution of origin.
5. **Craft.** Representing Nepal worldwide means the work has to be
   excellent — sloppy work done under a national flag embarrasses the
   flag. Tests green, docs honest, promises kept.

## Anti-patterns

- Costume branding: Nepali visuals over foreign-shaped product behavior.
- Claiming to represent Nepal before the product has served a single real
  Nepali merchant — the flag is earned by reality, not asserted.
- Diluting origin to look "global": global reach, Nepali roots. Never the
  reverse.
- Speaking for Nepal in marketing before merchants speak for Hami in
  practice.
