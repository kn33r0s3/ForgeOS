# Sprint Brief — First Rupee (for the human)

This is the one part of the sprint a human must do: talk to a real
seller. Everything else is prepared. One merchant, one week, one
measurable outcome.

## Who you're looking for

- A **social seller**: sells via TikTok / Facebook / WhatsApp, ideally
  Kathmandu valley (so you can meet if it works).
- **Visible strain signals:** "DM for price" with slow replies, stories
  saying "can't keep up," missed-call complaints, posts asking for
  helpers, HamroBazaar listings with "Call now" and multi-number blocks.
- **Not:** big brands, Daraz sellers, formal registered businesses.

## Where to look (public surfaces only — look, don't pitch yet)

- TikTok: Nepali seller hashtags, "order DM" captions, promo reels.
- Facebook: public buy/sell groups, Marketplace listings showing phone
  numbers (phone = identity — that's the tell).
- HamroBazaar: sellers defeating number-masking with raw numbers in
  descriptions — they're drowning in calls already.

## First contact — what to say

Honest, no pitch, no fake identity. Say who you are and that it's a
one-week test:

> "Hey — I see you sell through [TikTok/WhatsApp]. I'm testing an idea:
> for one week, I handle your customer replies fast. Free test — you
> only pay if it actually recovers sales. Want the 2-minute version?"

- If no reply in 2 days, move on. Never follow up twice, never spam.
- If they ask who you are: your real name, and "I'm working with Hami,
  a Kathmandu project studying how shops actually sell."

## The week protocol

1. They add you to their WhatsApp / TikTok DMs (their choice, their
   account — you never take over anything).
2. Agree hours (e.g., 9am–9pm). Reply within 15 minutes inside them.
3. **Log every inquiry** in the sheet below — no exceptions.
4. Never confirm what the seller can't deliver: check stock and price
   before saying yes to a customer.
5. You are "helping with replies this week" — never pretend to be
   their staff, never collect customer data beyond the sale.

## The log sheet (copy per day)

| # | Time in | Replied at | What they wanted | Outcome | Order value |
|---|---------|------------|------------------|---------|-------------|
| 1 |         |            |                  | recovered / lost / browsing | ₨ |
- **Recovered** = a sale that would have died without the fast reply.
  Be conservative: when in doubt, don't count it.
- **Lost** = they bought elsewhere, went silent, or the seller
  couldn't fulfill. Log the reason in one line.

## The price talk (end of week)

> "This week the fast replies recovered ₨X in sales that would have
> died. My cut is [10% / ₨Y fixed]. If you don't feel it was worth
> it, pay nothing."

- Outcome-based, always: no recovery → no charge.
- First rupee changes hands here — or the week ends at an honest
  zero, which is also a result. Report both back exactly as they
  happened.

## Report back

One page: who, what they sell, inquiries logged, recovered sales,
rupees recovered, rupees paid, what broke, one quote in their own
words (paraphrased ok, never invented). That page is worth more than
ten more research rounds.
