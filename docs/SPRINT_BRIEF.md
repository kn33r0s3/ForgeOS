# Sprint Brief — First Rupee (for the human)

This brief has two distinct stages: non-commercial discovery, then (only if separately cleared) an operational seller pilot. A consented discovery conversation does not authorize handling customer messages, promising a service, setting a price, or starting a paid sprint.

## Who you're looking for

- A **social seller**: sells via TikTok / Facebook / WhatsApp, ideally
  Kathmandu valley (so you can meet if it works).
- **Visible strain signals:** "DM for price" with slow replies, stories
  saying "can't keep up," missed-call complaints, posts asking for
  helpers, HamroBazaar listings with "Call now" and multi-number blocks.
- For an operational pilot, a seller must meet registration/listing requirements counsel confirms apply; do not exclude formally registered businesses.
- A seller with unregistered or unclear status may take part in non-commercial discovery only, not paid onboarding.
- A public phone number/profile is not consent to contact, access an account, copy customer data, or pitch Hami.

## Where to look (public surfaces only — look, don't pitch yet)

- TikTok: Nepali seller hashtags, "order DM" captions, promo reels.
- Facebook/TikTok: observe public listings only; do not scrape, harvest contact details, post in groups, or treat public listings as permission to contact.
- HamroBazaar: learn from public listings without copying masked or unmasked numbers into Hami.
- Prefer an owner-approved warm introduction. Do not disguise the reason for contact or pretend to be a customer.

## First contact — what to say

Honest, no pitch, no fake identity. Say who you are and that it's a
one-week test:

> "Namaste — I'm learning how online sellers manage customer inquiries
> and what makes it difficult to reply on time. [Mutual contact] suggested
> I ask you. Would you be open to a 10-minute conversation about your
> workflow? I'm not asking for account access or offering a service."

Use only after the owner authorizes this exact contact and a genuine warm introduction exists. If they do not reply or decline, stop; do not follow up. State your real identity and Hami's purpose. This message is discovery-only and does not start a pilot.

## The operational week — gated, not currently authorized

Do not begin these steps from this brief alone. Before a pilot, record seller eligibility/registration evidence as counsel directs; get qualified legal/accounting review of Hami's role, privacy/data handling, seller agreement, tax and payment model; receive the seller's written permission for the specific channel, voice and handling behavior; define customer-data minimization/retention; and obtain an explicit owner ASK for scope, price, terms, spend and each customer-facing action.

After that separate gate:
1. The seller grants access through an approved method; never request passwords or impersonate the seller.
2. Agree hours and what replies the human may send, including escalation and stock/price checks.
3. Record minimal inquiry/reply timestamps and outcome categories. Do not store customer message bodies, names, phone numbers, payment details or credentials unless a reviewed, consented necessity is documented.
4. Never confirm what the seller cannot deliver; require seller confirmation for stock, price and terms.
5. Identify yourself honestly as an authorized helper; never pretend to be staff unless that representation is explicitly approved and legally cleared.

## The log sheet (copy per day)

| # | Time in | Replied at | What they wanted | Outcome | Order value |
|---|---------|------------|------------------|---------|-------------|
| 1 |         |            |                  | recovered / lost / browsing | ₨ |
- **Recovered** = a sale that would have died without the fast reply.
  Be conservative: when in doubt, don't count it.
- **Lost** = they bought elsewhere, went silent, or the seller
  couldn't fulfill. Log the reason in one line.

## Price/fee discussion — blocked pending owner and legal clearance

Do not quote or agree to a percentage, fixed fee, “free test,” or other commercial term from this draft. The owner must approve exact price/revenue share through the existing ASK process after qualified legal/accounting review resolves eligibility, Hami's role, contract duties, privacy, tax and whether/where Hami can receive a fee. Until then there is no pricing conversation, fee request or payment path. An honest zero is the current commercial state, not evidence that a pilot ran.

## Report back

One page: who, what they sell, inquiries logged, recovered sales,
rupees recovered, rupees paid, what broke, one quote in their own
words (paraphrased ok, never invented). That page is worth more than
ten more research rounds.
