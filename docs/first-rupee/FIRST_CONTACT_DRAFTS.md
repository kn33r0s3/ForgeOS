# First-contact template — generic, owner-controlled

**Status: DRAFT ONLY — NOT SENT.** This file contains no prospect identity or source locator. It is not permission to contact anyone.

## English template

Namaste — I’m building Hami to understand how Nepali sellers handle customer inquiries. I’m not assuming anything is going wrong at your business. Would you be open to a brief conversation about how you handle new questions and orders, including what happens when you are busy? No offer or fee is involved in this research conversation. I will take notes only with your permission.

## Nepali template

नमस्ते — म नेपाली बिक्रेताहरूले ग्राहकका सोधपुछ र अर्डर कसरी सम्हाल्नुहुन्छ भनेर बुझ्न Hami बनाउँदै छु। तपाईंको व्यवसायमा समस्या छ भनेर म अनुमान गरिरहेको छैन। नयाँ सोधपुछ र अर्डरहरू अहिले कसरी सम्हाल्नुहुन्छ, विशेष गरी व्यस्त हुँदा के हुन्छ भन्नेबारे छोटो कुराकानी गर्न मिल्छ? यो अनुसन्धान कुराकानीमा कुनै प्रस्ताव वा शुल्क छैन। तपाईंको अनुमति भएमा मात्र म नोट राख्छु।

## Operating fences

- An owner must re-check the current source, identify the intended recipient, approve the exact message/channel, and authorize that single send through the existing authorization path.
- A public page, source URL, prior draft or historical standing-authorization note is not current permission.
- Never auto-send, follow up, or reply on an owner's behalf. No reply or a decline means stop.
- Do not ask for or copy customer identities, phone numbers, order records, screenshots, account access or other private data. Ask for voluntary, minimized counts and workflows only if needed.
- Record `REAL` only when the event actually occurred and its evidence exists. A draft, send attempt, silence or seller assertion is not a conversation, booking, recovered sale or payment.
